How EV is calculated

Each team's probability of reaching each stage is derived from Polymarket winner markets. EV = expected Group pts + expected Tournament pts + expected Entertainment bonus (uniform 1/48 pre-tournament). Teams without Polymarket markets use a small uniform prior.
